Account Deletion - Final Capsule

This page explains how users of the Final Capsule app can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Important: Account deletion permanently removes the user's account and associated account information from our system, subject to any information that may need to be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record purposes.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Open the Final Capsule app.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Open your profile/account settings.
  4. Select the Delete Account option.
  5. Confirm the account deletion request.

After confirmation, the account deletion process will be started. The user may also contact the app developer if assistance is required with the deletion process.

Data That Will Be Deleted

When an account is deleted, the following account-associated information may be deleted, where applicable:

  • User account and authentication information
  • Name and profile information
  • Mobile/phone number stored in the user profile
  • Session and registration information
  • College/institution information
  • Account-related application data associated with the user

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained when required for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or transaction purposes.

For example, transaction or payment-related records may be retained when necessary to maintain transaction records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Retention: If information is required to be retained for one of the reasons described above, it will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for that purpose and will then be deleted or anonymized where applicable.

Deletion Time

Account deletion requests are processed as soon as reasonably possible. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or systems until those backups are automatically overwritten according to the applicable retention cycle.

Need Help?

If you are unable to delete your account from the app or need help with an account deletion request, please contact the Final Capsule support team through the support option provided in the app.
