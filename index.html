Account Deletion - Final Capsule

This page explains how users of the Final Capsule app can delete their account and associated personal data.

Important: Deleting your account is permanent. Your account and associated personal information will be deleted, except for information that may need to be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or transaction-record purposes.

How to Delete Your Account

Users can delete their account directly from the Final Capsule app. No separate email or external account deletion request is required when the in-app deletion option is available.

  1. Open the Final Capsule app.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Open Profile.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Review the confirmation message.
  6. Confirm the account deletion.
After confirmation: The account deletion process will begin and the account will no longer be available for normal use.

Data That Will Be Deleted

When an account is deleted, the following account-associated information may be deleted:

  • User account and authentication information
  • Name and profile information
  • Mobile or phone number stored in the profile
  • Session and registration information
  • College or institution information
  • Other account-associated application data

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained when necessary for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or transaction purposes.

For example, transaction or payment-related records may be retained when necessary to maintain transaction records, prevent fraud, resolve payment or transaction disputes, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Retention: Information that is required to be retained will be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose. When it is no longer required, it will be deleted or anonymized where applicable.

Deletion Time

Account deletion is initiated after the user confirms the deletion request in the app. Account-associated data is processed for deletion as soon as reasonably possible.

Some information may temporarily remain in system backups until those backups are automatically overwritten according to the applicable backup retention cycle.

Need Help?

If you are unable to delete your account through the app or need assistance with account deletion, please contact the Final Capsule support team through the support option provided in the app.
