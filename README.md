<h1>Account Deletion - Final Capsule</h1>

<p>
This page explains how users of the <strong>Final Capsule</strong>
app can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.
</p>

<div class="notice">
<strong>Important:</strong>
Account deletion permanently removes the user's account and associated
account information, subject to information that may need to be retained
for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record purposes.
</div>

<h2>How to Request Account Deletion</h2>

<ol>
<li>Open the <strong>Final Capsule</strong> app.</li>
<li>Log in to your account.</li>
<li>Open your profile or account settings.</li>
<li>Select the <strong>Delete Account</strong> option.</li>
<li>Confirm the account deletion.</li>
</ol>

<p>
After confirmation, the account deletion process will begin.
If assistance is required, users may contact the app developer through
the support option provided in the app.
</p>

<h2>Data That Will Be Deleted</h2>

<p>
When an account is deleted, account-associated information may be deleted,
including:
</p>

<ul>
<li>User account and authentication information</li>
<li>Name and profile information</li>
<li>Mobile or phone number stored in the profile</li>
<li>Session and registration information</li>
<li>College or institution information</li>
<li>Other account-associated application data</li>
</ul>

<h2>Data That May Be Retained</h2>

<p>
Certain information may be retained when required for legitimate legal,
security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or transaction purposes.
</p>

<p>
For example, transaction or payment-related records may be retained when
necessary to maintain transaction records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud,
or comply with applicable legal requirements.
</p>

<div class="warning">
<strong>Retention:</strong>
Information that must be retained will be kept only for as long as
reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose and will then be deleted
or anonymized where applicable.
</div>

<h2>Deletion Time</h2>

<p>
Account deletion requests are processed as soon as reasonably possible.
Some information may temporarily remain in backups until those backups
are automatically overwritten according to the applicable retention cycle.
</p>

<h2>Need Help?</h2>

<p>
If you are unable to delete your account from the app or need assistance,
please contact the Final Capsule support team through the support option
provided in the app.
</p>
